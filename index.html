About the tools

Good to know.

Handy for quick checks and early screening, not a replacement for detailed assessment.

Free to use

No accounts or sign-ups. Just open a tool and use it.

Source-referenced

Based on published guidance and public data, such as CSIRO, BoM, NIWA, state noise guidelines and AASB S2.

Runs in your browser

What you enter stays on your device and isn't stored on a server.

Personal project, not affiliated with any employer. These tools are provided free for education, early-stage screening and awareness. They are not a substitute for professional advice, site-specific assessment or regulatory determinations, and should not be relied on for legislative or regulatory compliance. Some tools use free-tier hosting and mapping services and may occasionally be unavailable.
